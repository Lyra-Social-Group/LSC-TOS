# Global Privacy Policy

## 1. Overview & Scope
This Global Privacy Policy governs how **Lyra Social Group** collects, processes, stores, and protects data across all services, Discord communities, VRChat instances, web applications, and automated systems (including custom bots and integrations). 

We operate on a principle of **data minimization**—collecting only the minimum telemetry strictly necessary to maintain community safety, facilitate infrastructure operations, and provide functional community features.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Community & Discord Telemetry
* **User Identifiers:** Discord User IDs, current usernames, display names, avatar hashes, and assigned server roles.
* **Moderation Records:** Timestamps, reason codes, moderator IDs, and contextual log entries associated with formal warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, and ticket interactions.
* **Access Logs:** IP-less network routing logs generated when connecting to custom web services protected via Cloudflare.

### B. Automated Systems & Bot Data
* **Command Execution:** Command triggers, command parameters, target user IDs, server/guild IDs, channel IDs, and execution timestamps.
* **Diagnostic & Error Logs:** Technical stack traces and error event metrics recorded during system exceptions to maintain bot stability and performance.

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
* We **do not** collect, sell, lease, or monetize real-name identities, financial data, precise physical location data, or contact lists.
* We **do not** store private direct messages (DMs) between members or persistent voice channel audio streams.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Data collected across our services is utilized strictly for:
* **Community Safety & Enforcement:** Tracking moderation actions, identifying ban evasion attempts, and maintaining server integrity.
* **Infrastructure Reliability:** Monitoring bot performance, identifying API bottlenecks, and resolving code exceptions.
* **Service Functionality:** Processing interactive commands, custom role management, and virtual asset/bot interactions.

**We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade user data to advertisers, data brokers, or external commercial entities.**

## 4. Data Storage, Security & Subprocessors
* **Security Controls:** All stored telemetry resides within restricted cloud infrastructure protected by automated firewalls, strong key-based authentication, and strict least-privilege access controls restricted solely to authorized engineering personnel.
* **Subprocessors:** We utilize verified third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., Cloudflare, Discord API) solely for network routing, DDoS mitigation, and real-time message transport.

## 5. Third-Party Links & External Platforms
Our services and Discord channels may contain links to external web destinations, third-party VRChat assets, or external platforms. Lyra Social Group is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or data collection policies of external third-party services.

## 6. User Rights & Data Subject Requests
Under applicable data privacy frameworks, you have the right to request access to or deletion of your stored telemetry:
* **Right to Access:** You may request a copy of the specific telemetry data associated with your Discord User ID.
* **Right to Erasure:** You may request the permanent removal of non-essential account records and command history logs, provided such data is not required to maintain active community safety bans or prevent ongoing policy evasion.
