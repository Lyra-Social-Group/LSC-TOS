# Verification Policy

## 1. Purpose & Objectives
The Verification Policy establishes structured entry controls across **Lyra Social Group** Discord servers, VRChat instances, and digital platforms. Verification requirements serve as a primary layer of defense to protect community spaces from automated bot raids, malicious alt accounts, targeted harassment, and spam campaigns.

## 2. Verification Hierarchy & Tiers

Access to channels, voice hubs, and specialized community spaces is governed by the following verification levels:

| Tier Level | Requirements | Granted Access / Privileges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 0: Unverified** | Unverified or newly joined Discord account. | Read-only access to welcome, rules, and support ticket channels. |
| **Tier 1: Basic** | Discord account with verified email and/or phone number; account age > 5 days. | Unlocks general public text and voice channels after completing native Discord onboarding. |
| **Tier 2: Community Member** | Completed rules acknowledgment check and verified via community bot onboarding. | Full participation across primary chat channels, media sharing, and event voice hubs. |
| **Tier 3: Access Restricted** | Manual application or staff review (e.g., event organizers, creators, trusted roles). | Unlocks sensitive channels, specialized VRChat instance links, and administrative hubs. |

## 3. Automated Screening & Security Controls
* **Discord Safety Integration:** Our servers enforce Discord’s native high-security verification settings, requiring verified email/phone linked to accounts.
* **New Account Gatekeeping:** Accounts created within less than 24–72 hours may experience temporary holding periods before progressing past Tier 1 screening during heightened threat levels or raid alerts.
* **VPN & Proxy Filtering:** Automated bot services may flag or require additional verification steps for accounts connecting through known high-risk proxies or anonymizing networks during onboarding.

## 4. Manual Review Protocols
For restricted tiers or accounts flagged by automated threat filters:
* **Ticket Submission:** Users unable to pass automated checks may open a support ticket in `#verification-help` or contact moderation staff.
* **Staff Discretion:** Moderation staff reserve the right to perform brief manual reviews (evaluating account history, mutual servers, or VRChat profile links) prior to granting restricted tier roles.
* **No Rights Guarantee:** Verification is a security measure, not a guaranteed right; access may be denied if an account exhibits clear indicators of raid intent, ban evasion, or malicious automation.

## 5. Account Security & Verification Integrity
* **Transfer Prohibited:** Verification status and granted roles are strictly tied to individual accounts and cannot be transferred, sold, or shared.
* **Revocation:** Verification status may be temporarily revoked or downgraded if an account shows suspicious activity, compromise indicators, or violates community rules.
