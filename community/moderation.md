# Moderation Policy

## 1. Purpose & Core Objective
This Moderation Policy defines the operational protocols, enforcement matrices, and disciplinary procedures utilized across **Lyra Social Group** Discord servers, VRChat spaces, and community hubs. Our objective is to ensure consistent, fair, and transparent enforcement of community standards (`community/guidelines.md`) while maintaining a safe environment for all members.

## 2. Progressive Discipline Model
Moderation actions generally follow a structured progressive framework. However, moderation staff retain the authority to bypass lower tiers and apply immediate high-level penalties (such as permanent bans) depending on the severity, intent, and impact of the infraction.

* **Tier 1:** System Warning
* **Tier 2:** Timeout / Mute
* **Tier 3:** Kick / Temporary Ban
* **Tier 4:** Permanent Ban

| Enforcement Level | Description & Duration | Typical Applicability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: System Warning** | Formal warning logged via moderation bot. Serves as official notice. | Minor rule slips, off-topic chat clutter, initial noise violations. |
| **Tier 2: Timeout / Mute** | Temporary restriction from text/voice interaction (1 hour to 7 days). | Mild toxicity, continuous spam, persistent argumentativeness after warning. |
| **Tier 3: Kick / Temp Ban** | Removal from server with reentry permitted or timed ban (1 to 30 days). | Severe disruption, repeated policy violations, circumventing staff directives. |
| **Tier 4: Permanent Ban** | Permanent removal and blacklisting across all Lyra Social Group spaces. | Harassment, doxxing, hate speech, malicious exploits, raid involvement. |

## 3. Immediate Ban Triggers
To preserve community safety, the following infractions carry zero tolerance and will result in an immediate **Tier 4 Permanent Ban** without prior warning:
* **Severe Harassment & Doxxing:** Publishing non-public personal identification data, real names, or private media of any individual.
* **Malicious Exploits & Security Threats:** Distributing malware, IP loggers, phishing links, or executing platform attacks.
* **Hate Speech & Discrimination:** Direct slurs, targeted bigotry, or hate group propaganda.
* **Illegal Material:** Distributing or linking to illicit or non-consensual content.
* **Ban Evasion:** Using alternate accounts ("alts") or proxies to bypass active warnings, mutes, or bans.

## 4. Evidence & Logging Protocols
To ensure administrative accountability and support fair appeal proceedings (`community/appeals.md`):
* **Documentation:** Moderators must capture and archive clear context and evidence (e.g., raw message logs, screenshots, video capture, command audit trails) prior to or immediately following any formal mute, kick, or ban.
* **Audit Trail:** All actions must be registered in designated internal moderation log channels detailing the target user ID, issuing moderator ID, rule reference, reason code, and attached evidence.

## 5. Staff Discretion & Scope of Authority
While this document outlines standard operating procedures, non-standard situations may arise:
* **Reasoned Action:** Moderators are explicitly empowered to take swift corrective action on behaviors or situations not specifically enumerated within this policy if the conduct poses an immediate threat to member safety, server stability, or operational integrity.
* **Administrative Override:** Lyra Social Group Administration retains full authority to review, modify, reduce, or overturn any moderation decision executed by community staff.

## 6. Support & Disciplinary Inquiries
* **Enforcement Appeals:** Users subjected to formal disciplinary bans or mutes may submit an appeal by following the procedures outlined in `community/appeals.md`.
