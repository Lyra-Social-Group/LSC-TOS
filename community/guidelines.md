# Community Guidelines

## 1. Scope & Core Mission
These Community Guidelines govern user conduct, content sharing, and interactions across all **Lyra Social Group** digital platforms, including our Discord servers, VRChat instances, game servers, and social spaces. By participating in our community, you agree to adhere to these standards at all times.

---

## 2. Respect & Decorum
* **Zero Tolerance for Harassment:** Treat all members with basic courtesy and respect. Targeted abuse, personal attacks, harassment, doxxing, predatory behavior, and hate speech or bigotry of any kind are strictly forbidden and will result in immediate removal.
* **Topic Alignment:** Maintain channel integrity by keeping conversations relevant to the designated channel topics. Continuous disruption or intentional derailment of active discussions is prohibited.
* **Conflict De-escalation:** Avoid bringing external drama, public arguments, or witch-hunts into public channels. Disputes should be resolved privately or escalated to staff via our support ticket system.

---

## 3. Content & Media Standards
* **NSFW & Explicit Material:** Explicit, sexually suggestive, or NSFW content is strictly prohibited in general spaces and must remain confined exclusively to designated, age-gated channels (where applicable). Content violating applicable legal standards or platform Terms of Service is completely banned.
* **Malicious Content & Links:** Posting links to phishing schemes, malware, IP loggers, unauthorized software cracks, or exploit utilities is strictly forbidden.
* **Spam & Unsolicited Promotion:** Self-promotion, referral links, automated spam, and unsolicited server invitations—whether in public channels or sent via DM to members—are not allowed without prior administrative authorization.

---

## 4. Voice Hubs & Virtual Reality Spaces (VRChat / Audio)
* **Audio Etiquette:** Maintain clear audio levels in voice channels and active VRChat instances. Intentionally broadcasting ear-rape, loud background noise, mic spam, or unauthorized soundboard spam is prohibited.
* **Spatial & Personal Boundaries:** Respect personal space and boundaries within virtual reality environments. Invading personal space, utilizing crashers/lag avatars, or engaging in unwanted virtual physical contact will lead to swift moderation action.
* **Instance Rules:** When joining Lyra Social Group hosted VRChat instances or private lobbies, follow any additional event-specific directives communicated by instance hosts or staff members.

---

## 5. Enforcement & Support
Violations of these guidelines are managed under our structured [`community/moderation.md`](moderation.md) framework. Moderation staff retain full authority to issue warnings, timeouts, kicks, or permanent bans based on the severity of the infraction.

* **Reporting Violations:** Report policy breaches or unsafe behavior directly to our staff team by opening a support ticket in our primary Discord server.
