# Lyra Social Group LLC
# Employee & Contractor Agreement
## Media, Marketing, Community Management & Future Operational Roles

This Employee & Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") governs all individuals performing work on behalf of **Lyra Social Group LLC** ("Lyra Social Group", "LSG", "Company", "we", "our", or "us").

This Agreement applies to paid employees, interns, volunteers, contractors, freelancers, ambassadors, moderators, social media managers, content creators, designers, developers, editors, marketing staff, and future operational positions unless a separate written agreement supersedes this document.

By accepting a position or performing work for Lyra Social Group LLC, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Agreement.

---

# 1. Scope of Work

Personnel may be assigned responsibilities including, but not limited to:

- Social media management
- Community management
- Discord moderation
- VRChat community management
- Graphic design
- Video editing
- Photography
- Streaming
- Marketing campaigns
- Public relations
- Customer support
- Software development
- Website administration
- Documentation
- Event planning
- Talent management
- Partnership outreach
- Business development
- Administrative work
- Future operational roles created by Lyra Social Group LLC

Specific responsibilities may change as business needs evolve.

---

# 2. Employment Status

Unless otherwise stated in a separate written agreement:

- Employment is at-will where permitted by applicable law.
- Volunteers are not employees.
- Contractors are independent contractors.
- Nothing in this Agreement guarantees continued employment or future work.

Separate employment contracts, offer letters, or contractor agreements take precedence where applicable.

---

# 3. Professional Conduct

While representing Lyra Social Group, personnel are expected to:

- Act professionally.
- Treat community members respectfully.
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Follow internal policies.
- Communicate honestly.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Protect company assets.
- Follow applicable laws and platform policies.

Personnel represent Lyra Social Group whenever acting in an official capacity.

---

# 4. Social Media Standards

Personnel responsible for social media accounts shall:

- Represent the Lyra Social Group brand professionally.
- Verify information before posting.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Avoid posting misleading information.
- Clearly separate personal opinions from official company communications.
- Maintain brand consistency.
- Protect confidential business information.
- Avoid engaging in public arguments while representing the company.

Only authorized personnel may speak on behalf of Lyra Social Group.

---

# 5. Confidentiality

Personnel may receive confidential information including:

- Internal documentation
- Business strategies
- Financial information
- Customer information
- Staff information
- Security procedures
- Internal discussions
- Unreleased products
- Source code
- Administrative tools
- Marketing plans

Confidential information may not be disclosed without authorization unless required by law.

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment or affiliation ends.

---

# 6. Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

All work created within the scope of employment or contractual duties for Lyra Social Group becomes the property of Lyra Social Group LLC to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Graphics
- Logos
- Videos
- Social media posts
- Marketing campaigns
- Source code
- Documentation
- Website content
- Photography
- Audio
- Music
- Designs
- Written content

Where ownership does not automatically transfer under applicable law, you assign all transferable rights to Lyra Social Group LLC.

---

# 7. Company Accounts & Equipment

Personnel granted access to company systems must:

- Use strong passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
- Protect login credentials.
- Never share credentials without authorization.
- Report unauthorized access immediately.

Company accounts remain the exclusive property of Lyra Social Group LLC.

Access may be revoked at any time.

---

# 8. Security Responsibilities

Personnel must promptly report:

- Security incidents
- Data breaches
- Account compromise
- Phishing attempts
- Lost devices containing company information
- Unauthorized access
- Suspicious activity

Failure to report security issues may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 9. Acceptable Use

Company resources may not be used for:

- Illegal activity
- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Copyright infringement
- Malware distribution
- Personal commercial ventures without authorization
- Unauthorized political campaigning
- Activities that damage the company's reputation

---

# 10. Conflicts of Interest

Personnel must disclose any situation that could reasonably create a conflict between their personal interests and the interests of Lyra Social Group.

Examples include:

- Competing businesses
- Financial interests
- Personal relationships affecting decision-making
- Outside employment creating conflicts

---

# 11. Performance Expectations

Personnel are expected to:

- Complete assigned work.
- Meet reasonable deadlines.
- Maintain professional communication.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Collaborate respectfully.
- Notify supervisors of significant delays.

Performance expectations may vary by position.

---

# 12. Media & Public Communications

Only authorized representatives may:

- Issue official statements.
- Respond to media inquiries.
- Announce partnerships.
- Publish legal announcements.
- Represent company policy.

Unauthorized public statements made on behalf of the company are prohibited.

---

# 13. Data Protection

Personnel must comply with all applicable company policies regarding:

- Privacy
- Data retention
- Information security
- Customer confidentiality
- Community safety

Access to personal information must only occur where necessary to perform assigned duties.

---

# 14. Return of Company Property

Upon termination or request, personnel must promptly return:

- Company devices
- Access credentials
- Documents
- Media
- Files
- Physical equipment
- Company records

Digital access may be revoked immediately upon separation.

---

# 15. Termination

Lyra Social Group may suspend or terminate employment, volunteer positions, or contractor relationships in accordance with applicable law and any separate written agreements.

Termination does not remove continuing obligations relating to:

- Confidentiality
- Intellectual property
- Data protection
- Security
- Non-disclosure obligations

---

# 16. Policy Updates

Lyra Social Group may modify this Agreement periodically.

Personnel will be notified of material changes where appropriate.

Continued employment or engagement after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Agreement.

---

# 17. Related Policies

Personnel are expected to comply with all applicable company policies, including:

- Employee Handbook
- Code of Conduct
- Privacy Policy
- Data Retention Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Social Media Policy
- Confidentiality Policy
- Discord Community Guidelines
- VRChat Community Guidelines
