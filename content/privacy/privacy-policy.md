# Global Privacy Policy

Welcome to the **Lyra Social Group LLC** ("Lyra Social Group", "LSG", "we", "our", or "us") Global Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, protect, retain, and disclose information across all Lyra Social Group services, including but not limited to:

- Discord communities
- Discord bots
- VRChat communities and events
- Websites
- Web applications
- APIs
- Support systems
- Community infrastructure
- Internal management tools
- Future products and services operated by Lyra Social Group LLC

By using any Lyra Social Group service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

---

# 1. Our Privacy Principles

Lyra Social Group is committed to respecting user privacy.

We strive to:

- Collect only information necessary to operate our services.
- Protect information using reasonable security measures.
- Be transparent about our data practices.
- Never sell personal information.
- Give users reasonable control over their information where practical and legally permitted.

---

# 2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on which Lyra Social Group services you use.

## 2.1 Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information through:

- Support tickets
- Contact forms
- Appeals
- Staff applications
- Partnership applications
- Event registrations
- Community verification
- Bug reports
- Feedback forms
- Email correspondence

## 2.2 Discord Information

When interacting with our Discord communities or bots, we may process:

- Discord User IDs
- Usernames
- Display names
- Avatar hashes
- Server (Guild) IDs
- Channel IDs
- Role assignments

- Moderation history
- Warning records

When participating in Lyra Social Group VRChat communities or events, we may process:

When using our websites or web applications, we may collect:

- Device type

- IP address
- HTTP request logs
- Session identifiers
- Cookies (where applicable)
- Cloudflare security events

---

## 2.5 Diagnostic Information

To improve reliability and security, we may collect:

- Error logs
- Crash reports
- API failures
- Performance metrics
- Diagnostic telemetry
- Audit events

# 3. Information We Do Not Collect

Unless explicitly required for a service, we do not knowingly collect:

- Government-issued identification numbers
- Payment card information
- Banking information
- Contact lists
- Private Discord direct messages
- Private VRChat messages
- Voice communications outside services we directly operate
- Precise GPS location information

We do not sell or rent user information to advertisers, marketing companies, or data brokers.
