# Creator Guidelines

## 1. Scope & Purpose
These Creator Guidelines establish expectations and operational rules for content creators, streamers, artists, and community influencers operating within **Lyra Social Group** digital hubs, VRChat instances, and media platforms. We aim to support creators while protecting community privacy and brand integrity.

---

## 2. Content Creation, Streaming & Recording

### A. Broadcast Standards
* **Community Alignment:** Content recorded or broadcast live within official community spaces, VRChat instances, or voice channels must adhere to our core Community Guidelines (`community/guidelines.md`). Broadcasting explicit hate speech, harassment, or non-consensual graphic content while representing or occupying community spaces is strictly prohibited.
* **Privacy Controls:** Creators must take reasonable precautions to avoid displaying private staff logs, moderation tickets, user support discussions, or non-public personal information on stream or in uploaded video content.
* **In-Game Overlay Management:** When streaming inside public or semi-private community channels, creators should configure stream overlays (e.g., chat logs, notification popups) to prevent leaking private or sensitive communications.

### B. On-Air Disruption & Collaboration
* **Stream Sniping & Harassment:** Stream sniping, intentional lobby crashing, or targeted harassment of featured creators or event hosts will result in immediate removal and permanent account blacklisting across all Lyra Social Group spaces.
* **Co-Streaming & Branding:** Creators officially affiliated with or co-hosting events alongside Lyra Social Group must coordinate with the Events Team prior to using official branding, overlay assets, or logos in their broadcasts.

---

## 3. Self-Promotion & Advertising

### A. Designated Promotion Spaces
* **Channel Restrictions:** Promotion of personal social media channels, Twitch/YouTube streams, Patreon accounts, personal stores, or creative portfolios is strictly confined to explicitly marked self-promotion channels (e.g., `#self-promotion` or `#creator-showcase`).
* **Cross-Posting & Spam:** Unsolicited promotional messaging, multi-channel spamming, or mass DM advertising to community members is strictly forbidden.

### B. Commercial & Asset Promotion
* **Art & Avatar Commissions:** Artists, 3D modellers, and avatar creators sharing promotional material or accepting commissions must ensure their work complies with copyright, intellectual property standards, and platform-specific licensing terms (e.g., VRChat SDK terms).
* **Affiliate & Referral Links:** Unauthorized commercial affiliate links, crypto/NFT marketing, or multi-level schemes are not permitted under any circumstances.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Compliance
Violations of this policy may lead to the removal of creator roles, deletion of promotional media, revocation of event stream access, or further disciplinary action as outlined in our [`community/moderation.md`](moderation.md) framework.
