# Appeals Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Appeals Policy outlines the official protocols and review procedures for individuals seeking to challenge disciplinary actions—including server mutes, role restrictions, kicks, and permanent bans—issued across **Lyra Social Group** platforms. We are committed to providing a fair, objective, and transparent mechanism to re-evaluate enforcement decisions.

---

## 2. Appeal Eligibility & Waiting Periods

### A. General Eligibility
* **Standard Ban Appeals:** Members subject to a permanent ban or major access restriction are eligible to submit a formal appeal after a **14-day waiting period** from the date the disciplinary action was enacted. Appeals submitted prior to the expiration of this 14-day period will be automatically rejected.
* **Temporary Actions:** Timeouts, temporary mutes (under 14 days), and minor warnings are generally managed through standard support channels rather than formal ban appeal procedures unless clear staff misconduct is alleged.

### B. Ineligible Infractions (Automatic Rejection)
To protect community safety and regulatory compliance, appeals submitted for actions resulting from severe zero-tolerance infractions (`community/moderation.md`) will be denied automatically without formal review. Non-appealable infractions include, but are not limited to:
* Doxxing, publishing private real-world information, or targeted real-life harassment.
* Distribution of malware, malicious software, IP loggers, or participating in cyberattacks/raids.
* Distribution or promotion of illegal material or severe hate speech.
* Exploitative or predatory behavior toward community members.

---

## 3. Appeal Submission & Review Workflow

### A. Submission Protocol
1. **Official Form:** Appeals must be submitted strictly through the official appeal form or by emailing [LEGAL@LYRAFOXWOOD.APP](mailto:LEGAL@LYRAFOXWOOD.APP) with the subject line `Formal Ban Appeal - [Discord User ID / Username]`.
2. **Required Information:** Submissions must include the user's registered ID/username, approximate date of action, the reason provided upon removal, and a clear explanation of why the restriction should be reconsidered (including steps taken to rectify past behavior).

### B. Review & Investigation
* **Objective Reviewer:** All appeals are assigned to an un-involved senior staff member or member of the Lyra Social Group Administration who was not directly involved in issuing the original penalty.
* **Evidence Audit:** The reviewing authority will examine internal moderation logs, archived evidence files, transcript records, and the appellant's statement to determine whether policy was applied correctly.

### C. Determinations & Finality
* **Possible Outcomes:** Appeals may result in a full reinstatement, conditional reinstatement (probationary period), reduction of penalty duration, or complete denial.
* **Final Authority:** Decisions rendered by the Lyra Social Group Appeals Board or Senior Administration are **final**. Repeating or spamming appeal forms following a final denial will result in a permanent block of communication channels.
