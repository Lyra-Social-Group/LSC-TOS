# Staff Code of Conduct

## 1. Scope & Applicability
This Staff Code of Conduct applies to all moderators, administrators, community managers, trial staff, and technical infrastructure operators (collectively, "Staff Members") within **Lyra Social Group**. Holding a staff position is a privilege, not a title of status. Staff members are held to a higher standard of conduct both within public channels and behind the scenes.

## 2. Core Operational Standards

### A. Professionalism & Neutrality
* **Objectivity:** Staff members must remain calm, neutral, and fair when resolving disputes, handling tickets, or enforcing server rules. 
* **Conflict of Interest:** Moderators must recuse themselves from handling tickets, reports, or disciplinary decisions involving close personal friends, partners, or individuals with whom they have an active personal dispute.
* **Representation:** When acting in an official capacity or wearing staff roles, your actions reflect directly on Lyra Social Group. Public toxicity, baiting, or escalating drama will not be tolerated.

### B. Confidentiality & Privacy Integrity
* **Internal Logs & Data:** Audit logs, ticket transcripts, internal staff discussions, telemetry data, and private user reports are strictly confidential.
* **Non-Disclosure:** Sharing screenshots, quoting internal staff channels, leaking upcoming project updates, or disclosing personal user data to non-staff members constitutes a critical security breach and will result in immediate termination of staff status.

### C. Accountability & Power Usage
* **Proportional Enforcement:** Moderation actions (warnings, mutes, kicks, bans) must follow established guidelines (`community/moderation.md`) and be backed by documented evidence.
* **Prohibition of Abuse:** Unwarranted kicks or bans, misuse of administrative bot commands, vanity role manipulation, or privilege escalation for personal amusement are strictly prohibited.
* **Transparency:** All formal disciplinary actions taken against users must be logged in the designated internal channels with appropriate reason codes and evidence.

## 3. Escalation & Conflict Resolution
* If a staff member believes a moderation decision was handled incorrectly by a peer, the issue must be discussed privately within internal staff channels or escalated to Administration. Publicly arguing with or overriding another staff member's actions in member-facing channels is strictly forbidden.
* Community complaints regarding staff misconduct are handled directly by Administration via private ticket or email.

## 4. Disciplinary Procedures & Removal
Failure to adhere to this Code of Conduct will result in immediate administrative action, structured as follows based on severity:
1. **Formal Verbal/Internal Warning:** Minor infractions or lapses in protocol.
2. **Temporary Suspension:** Role removal and pause of administrative duties pending review.
3. **Permanent Removal & Blacklist:** Immediate demotion, revocation of all access privileges, and potential blacklist from community spaces for severe breaches (e.g., leaks, deliberate abuse of power, harassment).
