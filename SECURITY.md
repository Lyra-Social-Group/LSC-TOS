# Security Policy

## Reporting Vulnerabilities
If you discover a security vulnerability within our Discord bots, web services, or infrastructure, please report it directly to administration.

**Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until it has been reviewed and addressed.**

### Submission Channels
* **Private Ticket:** Open a ticket in our primary Discord server.
* **Direct Email:** Contact the engineering team.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and provide updates on resolution timelines.
